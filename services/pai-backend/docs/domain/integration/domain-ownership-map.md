# INTEGRATION-01A — Domain Ownership Map

This map assigns ownership by business meaning, not by matching names, tables, or implementation folders. The current PAI source baseline is the repository root on `khanhtruong`; the historical INTEGRATION-00 artifacts were generated in the `pr-012-instructional-design-core` worktree and are treated as prior-scan evidence.

## Ownership matrix

| Entity | Semantic Owner | Data Owner | Lifecycle Owner | Integration Strategy | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| User / login account | LMS | LMS | LMS | Reference | LMS owns registration, password, JWT, email verification and account role. PAI receives a trusted actor reference; it must not duplicate credentials. |
| Actor authorization context | PAI | PAI projection/reference | PAI | Shared contract | PAI needs actor UUID, organization scope, role/delegation and validity for competency decisions. The contract must be derived from trusted LMS identity, not client claims. |
| Organization / Tenant | UNDECIDED | UNDECIDED | UNDECIDED | Shared contract | PAI requires organization isolation; target currently has no organization model. Do not map one LMS User to one tenant by assumption. |
| CV / JD Document | PAI | PAI | PAI | Own | PAI owns quarantine, retention, extraction eligibility, source locators and document kind. It is not the LMS training `Document`. |
| CandidateProfile | PAI | PAI | PAI | Own | Versioned extraction projection from CV evidence; not an LMS User profile. |
| Evidence | PAI | PAI | PAI | Reference | Source-backed claim with locator, excerpt, confidence and verification status. LMS may receive opaque evidence references only when needed. |
| RoleCompetencyProfile | PAI | PAI | PAI | Own | Approved role requirements, observable behaviors, rubric/evidence constraints and exact semantic policy binding. |
| Capability / Skill | PAI | PAI | PAI | Own | Semantic capability identifiers and provisional/verified status; target course categories are not substitutes. |
| GapAnalysis / CapabilityGapPortfolio | PAI | PAI | PAI | Own | Evidence gap and target-specific analysis; never replaced by enrollment status or course completion. |
| LearningObjective | PAI | PAI | PAI | Projection | PAI owns measurable competency-aligned objective semantics; LMS may store a delivery-facing copy with immutable source reference. |
| CourseBlueprint | PAI | PAI | PAI | Projection | Approved instructional design output containing objective/module/prerequisite/provenance metadata. It is not the published LMS Course. |
| ModuleBlueprint / LessonBlueprint | PAI | PAI | PAI | Projection | PAI owns instructional structure and alignment; LMS creates delivery content records from it. |
| Prerequisite graph | PAI | PAI | PAI | Projection | PAI owns dependency meaning and minimality. LMS may enforce delivery ordering but cannot redefine semantic prerequisites. |
| LearningDesignPolicy | PAI | PAI | PAI | Own | Quality gates, workload, practice coverage and policy/version constraints for generated learning design. |
| Delivery Course | LMS | LMS | LMS | Own | Published learning product and enrollment container. It may carry `source_blueprint_id/version` but not absorb blueprint semantics. |
| Delivery Module / Lesson | LMS | LMS | LMS | Own | Content delivery, ordering, assets and learner visibility. |
| Delivery Assessment / Quiz | LMS | LMS | LMS | Separate entity | LMS owns question publication, learner attempts, scoring and pass/fail. It may be generated from a PAI AssessmentBlueprint. |
| Competency AssessmentTemplate / AssessmentSubmission | PAI | PAI | PAI | Projection/reference | PAI owns rubric, evidence linkage, SME review and competency-decision eligibility. A target QuizAttempt is not a replacement. |
| Enrollment | LMS | LMS | LMS | Own | Self/assigned enrollment and course/path membership. |
| Progress / Completion | LMS | LMS | LMS | Shared contract | LMS owns lesson progress and completion status. A versioned LearningResult can be sent to PAI as evidence input, never as automatic verification. |
| LearningResult | SHARED_CONTRACT | LMS | LMS | Shared contract | Stable event/contract for course, lesson or quiz outcome. PAI consumes it only under explicit policy. |
| Course Certificate | LMS | LMS | LMS | Own | Course-completion artifact generated by LMS rules/templates. It is not a PAI competency credential. |
| Competency Credential | PAI | PAI | PAI | Reference | Policy-gated credential requiring competency decision, evidence and validity. LMS may display or link to verification. |
| Notifications / reports | LMS | LMS | LMS | Own | Operational LMS delivery/reporting; PAI audit remains authoritative for intelligence decisions. |
| ModelGateway / PrivacyGateway | PAI | PAI | PAI | Keep separate | PAI controls model routing, structured output and privacy policies. No direct LMS provider call. |

## Non-merge decisions

- `CourseBlueprint` and LMS `Course` are separate entities connected by a versioned projection reference.
- PAI `AssessmentTemplate`/`AssessmentDecision` and LMS `Quiz`/`QuizAttempt` are separate bounded concepts.
- PAI `Credential` and LMS `Certificate` are separate even though both may be shown to a learner.
- PAI CV/JD `Document` and LMS training-resource `Document` must not share a table or endpoint without an explicit new contract.
- PAI `CandidateProfile`, `Evidence`, `Competency` and `CapabilityGapPortfolio` do not map to target `User`, `Progress` or `Enrollment`.
